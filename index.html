About

I’m an M.Sc. student at the University of Tehran studying how neural systems learn and represent information, from cultured neurons on electrode arrays to monkey inferotemporal and prefrontal cortex.

I work in the Brain Computing Lab with Prof. M.-R. A. Dehaqani, and I compare what I see in brains with what emerges in artificial networks, using each to inform the other.

Research

Closed-loop tuning of IT neurons

Monkey recordings with closed-loop experiments I designed. Optimizing in the latent space of a face generative model (AAM) evolves images toward what each neuron prefers. This is my M.Sc. thesis.

Closed loop · Generative models · IT cortex

Prefrontal cortex in visual processing

How prefrontal cortex contributes to vision beyond the ventral pathway, studied with IT and PFC recordings during a task.

PFC · Ventral pathway · Neural coding

Information capacity of cultured neurons

How electrical stimulation is represented in primary rat neuronal cultures on MEAs, and how much information these networks can carry.
